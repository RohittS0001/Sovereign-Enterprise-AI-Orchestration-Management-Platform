# Gateway Vendor Contract Summary (Confidential)

## Service levels

The vendor guarantees 99.95% monthly availability for the capture API and a p99 latency
under 800 ms. Timeouts on more than 1% of requests in a day count as an SLA breach.

## Penalties

Each SLA breach earns a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee, capped at 30%. Credits must
be claimed in writing within 30 days.

## Exit clause

Three SLA breaches in a quarter allow termination with 60 days' notice and no exit fee.
